## Oracle sizing (SPEC.ja.md 2)

One-factor sweep: `sum(sites x candidates) + 1`, with 6 function-attribute candidates on each of 15 marked functions and 11 loop candidates on each loop site. At 2.5 min per fat-LTO + PGO build and 2.0 min of timing (n=15, 3 cases) an arm costs 4.5 min.

| rule (cumulative, pre-registered, result-blind) | loop sites | builds | wall clock |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| every loop site its own arm (upper bound; unreachable, because a plan addresses keys) | 149 | 1730 | 129.8 h |
| one arm per distinct site key | 127 | 1488 | 111.6 h |
| drop sites the training profile never entered | 62 | 773 | 58.0 h |
| drop trip count < 2 | 32 | 443 | 33.2 h |
| top 3 by hotness per mark | 16 | 267 | 20.0 h |

The function-attribute half is fixed at **90 builds** (6.8 h) whatever the cap does, and decision 61 (b) runs it first: the loop keys have to be re-dumped from the IR the chosen attributes produced, so the loop half of the sweep is priced against a site list that does not exist until the attribute phase is over.
